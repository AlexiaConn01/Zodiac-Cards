This is my first official balatro mod, so be kind and if you have any feedback or suggestions please let me know 

Zodiac Cards adds a new consumable type, Zodiac Cards, which range in effects from giving jokers new editions, or permanantly improving one your stats, and also adds a few other related items, such as booster packs

Instalation is the same as most other mods 

I made most of the art and assets myself, apart from the background of the Zodiac Cards which my friend zeRigit21 made.
